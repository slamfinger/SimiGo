## 1. Coverage rule

This map answers whether a research area can be recovered, not whether its
conclusion is true. Symbols mean:

```text
✓             explicit coverage exists at that layer
PARTIAL       important material exists, but recovery remains incomplete
HISTORICAL    intentionally retained as closed/paused evidence
NEEDS REVIEW  census-visible but not yet line-level summarized
UNKNOWN       this map preserves the absence as unknown
```

No symbol promotes an idea, prediction, proposal, or prepared design into a
result or decision. If this map conflicts with L2 or State, L2/State wins.
