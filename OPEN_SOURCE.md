# SimiGo-Lab Open Source Boundary

SimiGo-Lab is the research and evidence repository for the frozen Execution
State Contract and its conformance/reference work.

## Asset layers

| Layer | Access | License | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contract specifications, gate records, research documents, experiment evidence | Open | CC-BY-4.0 | Attribution; no endorsement |
| Runtime contract source, experimental runtime, adapters, conformance suites | Open | Apache-2.0 | Commercial use and independent implementations are allowed |
| Official product app, hosted/enterprise products, management plane, support | Separate project or future commercial terms | Not granted here | Product boundary remains reserved |

## Repository rules

- This repository contains no model weights.
- Experimental evidence is published only after local paths, usernames, signing
  team identifiers, and machine-private identifiers are redacted.
- Opening research material does not broaden a bounded experimental claim into
  a universal, backend-independent, or mathematically minimal claim.
- The Contract text is open, but changing its semantic membership or lifecycle
  obligations requires a separate Closure Revision Gate.
